Why N/A

What we leave out matters as much as what we put in.

No Palm Oil

Environmental impact aside, palm oil masks flavor and creates a waxy mouthfeel. We let peanuts be peanuts.

No Refined Sugar

White sugar adds sweetness but strips complexity. Dates bring mineral-rich sweetness with character.

No Emulsifiers

Lecithin and mono/diglycerides prevent separation — but they also dull flavor. Natural separation is a sign of honesty.

No Stabilizers

Hydrogenated oils keep texture uniform. We prefer stone-grinding patience over chemical shortcuts.

No Artificial Flavors

"Natural flavor" can mean anything. Our flavor comes from roasting, grinding, and ingredient quality.

No Preservatives

Small batches. Proper sealing. Natural shelf stability from low water activity. No benzoates or sorbates.

Our Story

Started from a simple question.

Why does every peanut butter jar contain ingredients we can't pronounce? Palm oil. Hydrogenated oils. Refined sugar. Emulsifiers. Stabilizers. Preservatives.

We wanted a peanut butter that tasted like peanuts — roasted, rich, alive — sweetened only by the caramel warmth of dates. Nothing to hide. Nothing to apologize for.

NO ADDITIVES (N/A) was born from that refusal to compromise. Small batches. Honest ingredients. A label you can read in three seconds.
